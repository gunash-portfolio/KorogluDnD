# Problem and outcome

Source: `KorogluEpicGameProposal.md`. Canonical tale: [Epic of Koroghlu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Epic_of_Koroghlu).

## The specific problem

Turkic epics such as Koroglu are still taught mostly as **passive text or lecture**. Listeners do not *do* the moral choice, the mounted fight, or the ashik’s verse, so the story stays distant and easy to forget.

That is a **heritage-access problem**, not a missing encyclopedia page. The dastan is already public. What is missing is a way to **practice** its values: justice over revenge, protecting the weak, poetry as courage.

## The user and the problem

Judging prompt: who this is for, and what goes wrong for them today. “Everyone” is too vague.

This game is **not** for “all culture lovers.” It is for three people who already bump into Koroglu and then lose him.

**1. A 15–18 year-old in an Azerbaijani, Turkish, or Turkmen classroom (or diaspora Saturday school).**  
They know the name from a statue, a test, or a grandparent’s radio. What goes wrong: the dastan arrives as a long recitation or a Wikipedia plot. They never *ride*, never choose justice over revenge, never shout a verse. They close the tab. Retention dies because there was no action.

**2. A heritage or literature teacher with one short period.**  
They cannot assign a 400-page epic and still prove the ethics. What goes wrong: they show a slide titled “Epic of Koroglu” and hope a summary sticks. They have no 2-minute artifact where a student *does* Ali Kishi’s truth, the ashik’s saz, Nigar’s equality, and the gate of Çamlıbel.

**3. A young player who already games — but only generic fantasy.**  
They will spend an hour on a horse-and-sword loop. What goes wrong today: that hour is spent in a made-up kingdom, never in their own oral epic. The culture sits unused next to the habit that could carry it.

The encyclopedia page already exists. The failure is **practice**: no mounted choice, no poetry-as-courage, no moral fork they can fail. This prototype is the short ride those three people are missing.

## The clear outcome of solving it

Players **act** the MVP loop instead of reading it:

1. Father blinded for telling the truth  
2. Recruit ally minds (Deli Hasan, Ayvaz)  
3. Mounted combat (saber + verse)  
4. Rescue Nigar  
5. Take Çamlıbel for the just  

**Outcome to claim:** culture sticks because it was **played**. The proposal’s benchmark is higher retention versus text-only learning (heritage-game engagement research cited in the proposal). In this build, success is visible: an ally joins, a verse slows a tyrant’s rider, the ashik heals those who stand with song, Nigar is freed, the gate opens.

## What this prototype proves

| Problem symptom | Playable answer |
| --- | --- |
| Epic as wall of text | Two-card open, then ride the valley |
| No agency | Sword, verse, who you ride toward |
| No moral stake | Father + elder: justice is not revenge |
| Combat without culture | Ashik saz heals and demoralizes |

If judges remember one sentence: **the problem is passive heritage; the outcome is a playable Koroglu who wins by justice, not by glory.**

# Two-minute playthrough (lore + gameplay)

Record the live game. Do not pause on menus after the first two character cards. Click only if auto-ride stalls.

## 0:00–0:06 — Opening

- Card 1: Ali Kishi blinded for telling the truth about the colt Kırat.
- Card 2: You are Koroglu. Saber, verse, justice — not glory.
- The valley loads. Koroglu rides on his own toward Ali Kishi.

## 0:06–0:20 — Hear the wounded truth

- Stay on Ali Kishi until his line plays (bravery yoked to goodness).
- Ride the ashik. Health bars should rise while you stand near him (saz heals).
- Optional: one Verse shout so poetry combat is on camera.

## 0:20–0:40 — Episode I: tax riders

- Ride east. Two collectors of Bolu Bey. Sword and verse until both fall.

## 0:40–1:05 — Gather the band

- Ride Ayvaz until he joins (ally AI).
- Deli Hasan should already be fighting or covering you.
- Show the captain and two guards closing. Sword once or twice.

## 0:50–1:20 — Mounted fight

- Keep the camera on Koroglu vs the captain.
- Mix Sword and Verse. Enemies should slow when verses land.
- If HP drops, ride back to the ashik, heal, return.

## 1:20–1:45 — Rescue

- When the captain falls, Nigar walks toward you.
- Ride to her. Let her line play (kindness is courage).

## 1:45–2:00 — Çamlıbel

- Ride the fortress gate (turns green after the captain falls).
- End on the embrace still, then the Çamlıbel victory lines.

After the two opening cards the game rides itself (father → ashik heal → Ayvaz → verse and saber on the captain) until you click. Use that auto-ride for a short clip; use the full timing above for the two-minute cut.
